About the Protocols

Pub-Sub
MQTT

Lightweight publish-subscribe protocol designed for constrained devices and low-bandwidth, high-latency networks. Ideal for IoT sensor telemetry.

RESTful
CoAP

Specialized web transfer protocol for use with constrained nodes and networks in IoT environments. RESTful design optimized for machine-to-machine communication.

Industrial
OPC-UA

Platform-independent service-oriented architecture for industrial automation. Provides secure, reliable data exchange between industrial devices and enterprise systems.
